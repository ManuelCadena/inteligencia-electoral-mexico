Part V

Integration — capstones and experimental design

The channels combine by late fusion; evaluation honestly compares each channel against persistence and against the model without that channel (ablation).

V.1Capstone C2 — multimodal late-fusion architecture

Late fusion keeps each modality's encoder frozen and learns only the combination — unlike early fusion (concatenating raw features) it avoids forcing incompatible geometries into one space, and unlike joint end-to-end training it keeps every branch independently auditable. The four branches (tabular MLP, temporal LSTM, spatial GNN, textual BETO) each emit a fixed-length embedding; concatenation plus election/entity embeddings feeds a fusion MLP. P

Mandatory ablation matrix: tabular-only · +temporal · +spatial · +text · full. The paper reports each channel's Δ over baseline — including when it is negative. P

V.2Capstone C3 — turnout forecasting

The simplest end-to-end experiment: predict next-cycle PARTICIPACION per unit (turnout is continuous, interpretable, non-compositional — avoiding the simplex's complexity). MLP (III.1) vs LSTM (III.9) vs persistence. It fixes the evaluation harness for all later experiments. Sprint 1 = this notebook. P

V.3Capstone C1 — municipal GNN

Graph Neural Networks replace a grid with an explicit adjacency graph: municipalities as nodes, edges = INEGI geographic adjacency (pending integration) or socioeconomic similarity as documented fallback. GCN (Graph Convolutional Network, Kipf & Welling 2017) averages a node's neighborhood embedding; GraphSAGE samples and aggregates neighborhoods; GAT (Graph Attention, Veličković et al. 2018) weighs neighbors by learned attention. Messages = share lags + macro_lag + VAE latent embedding (III.6). SQ3 asks whether spatial diffusion reduces error in local deputies — where geography is the richest available structure. P

Blocking dependency

Requires the INEGI municipal adjacency/shapefile — open gap (VI.4). Declared fallback: k-NN graph by centroid distance or feature similarity.

V.4Capstone C4 — electoral-anomaly forensics

Autoencoder outliers (III.6) + FLAG_CONSISTENCIA + temporal-model residuals form an electoral-anomaly detection system: municipalities whose voting pattern is explained by neither their latent profile nor their history. Inspired by Cantú's (2014) forensic analysis of fraudulent fingerprinting — reported as a statistical anomaly, never as a fraud accusation without external evidence. Reporting discipline: statistical anomaly ≠ legal irregularity. P

V.5Capstone C5 — textual nowcasting

90-day pre-election window: textual features only (IV.7, IV.9, IV.10) + recent macro → outcome prediction. Answers whether discourse carries anticipatory signal — and delimits what "anticipatory" means: a mañanera of day T can only explain elections after T. P
