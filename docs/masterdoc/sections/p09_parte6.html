ThreatMechanismMitigation implemented/planned
Temporal leakagefeature from the election year or later _LAG1 rule; text publication date; harness tests D
Ecological fallacyinferring individual behavior from aggregates explicit declaration: models predict aggregates, not voters P
Text selection biasmañaneras = incumbent-only discourse post-2018 corpus documented as "federal-government discourse", never "public opinion" P
Regime change1991–2017 regularities may not hold after 2018 test includes the MORENA era; temporal-weighting ablation P
Small n~6–8 presidential cycles per unit hierarchical pooling, strong regularization, bootstrap CIs P

VI.3Execution plan — 6 sprints

  1. Sprint 1 — Harness and baseline: notebooks 02 (harness), 03 (MLP), C3 (turnout). Deliverable: PyTorch pipeline + first honest result vs persistence.
  2. Sprint 2 — Spatial structure: 04 CNN (option a), 06 VAE, C1 GNN with INEGI adjacency. Deliverable: latent map + spatial model.
  3. Sprint 3 — Textual corpus: mañaneras integration, III.8 ASR, n01–n04 (BoW, LSTM, tokenizer, n-grams). Deliverable: mananeras_asr.parquet + textual baseline.
  4. Sprint 4 — Deep NLP: n05–n12 (embeddings, t-SNE, LDA, STM, stance, NER, CRF, BETO). Deliverable: persisted textual features.
  5. Sprint 5 — Integration: V.1 multimodal, III.13–14 generative/SSM, C4–C5. Deliverable: complete V.7 table + ablations.
  6. Sprint 6 — Paper: writing on real results, final figures, reproducibility package. Deliverable: manuscript.
